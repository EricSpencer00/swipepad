  1. Install

    Download the app from the releases page. Right-click it and choose Open the first time.

  2. Allow Accessibility

    System Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Accessibility. Turn on SwipeType. Open SwipeType's setup window and click Recheck.

  3. Click into a text field

    Any app works. Web editors such as Claude's composer work too.

  4. Start swiping

    Double-tap ⌘. The keyboard appears on screen.

  5. Swipe a word

    Put one finger on the trackpad near the first letter, slide across the letters, and lift. The word is typed followed by a space. You don't need to land exactly on the first letter.

  6. Fix a word

    Press Delete to remove the last word. SwipeType offers the next best guess, and you can swipe again or tap a guess.

  7. Stop

    Press Esc or double-tap ⌘ again.

Pad area

Menu bar, then Pad Area. Full Trackpad uses the whole pad. iPhone-Sized Area uses a centred area the size of an iPhone keyboard. Compact Area is smaller still. Touches outside the area are ignored, so your palm can rest on the pad.

Tips

  • Swipe with one finger. Two fingers cancel the swipe.
  • A brief pause mid-swipe keeps the same word going.
  • Short words like "hi" work. If a word comes out wrong, press Delete.
